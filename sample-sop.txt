Standard Operating Procedure
Quality Control Testing for Pharmaceutical Products

SOP ID: SOP-QC-001
Version: 1.0
Effective Date: January 5, 2026
Department: Quality Control
Author: QC Manager

1. PURPOSE
This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) describes the procedures for quality control testing of pharmaceutical products to ensure they meet established specifications and regulatory requirements.

2. SCOPE
This procedure applies to all quality control personnel in the testing laboratory who are responsible for performing physical, chemical, and microbiological testing of raw materials, in-process materials, and finished pharmaceutical products.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES

3.1 QC Manager
- Oversees all testing activities and ensures compliance with this SOP
- Reviews and approves all test results
- Ensures proper training of QC personnel
- Maintains testing equipment and calibration schedules

3.2 QC Analyst
- Performs testing procedures according to approved methods
- Documents all test results accurately and completely
- Reports out-of-specification results immediately
- Maintains cleanliness and organization of testing area

3.3 QA Manager
- Reviews and approves final test reports
- Conducts periodic audits of testing procedures
- Ensures regulatory compliance
- Approves deviations and investigations

4. PROCEDURE

4.1 Sample Receipt and Handling
4.1.1 Upon receipt of samples:
- Verify sample identification matches the sample request form
- Check sample container integrity for any damage or contamination
- Record sample temperature if temperature-sensitive
- Log sample information in the Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)
- Assign unique sample identification number
- Store samples under appropriate conditions (2-8°C for refrigerated items, room temperature for stable items)

4.1.2 Sample Storage Requirements:
- Refrigerated samples: 2-8°C
- Frozen samples: -20°C or below
- Room temperature samples: 15-25°C
- Light-sensitive samples: Store in amber containers or dark area

4.2 Testing Procedures

4.2.1 Pre-Testing Requirements:
- Review and understand the approved test method
- Ensure all required equipment is available and calibrated
- Verify reagents are within expiry dates
- Wear appropriate Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
- Document environmental conditions (temperature, humidity)

4.2.2 Testing Execution:
- Follow approved test methods exactly as written
- Use only calibrated and qualified equipment
- Record all observations in real-time
- Perform duplicate or triplicate testing when required by the method
- Include appropriate controls (positive and negative)
- Never backdate or pre-date entries

4.2.3 Calculations and Data Recording:
- Perform all calculations using validated formulas
- Have a second analyst verify complex calculations
- Record all raw data in permanent ink
- Cross out errors with a single line and initial
- Never use correction fluid or erase entries

4.3 Documentation Requirements

4.3.1 Test Records Must Include:
- Sample identification and batch number
- Test method reference and version
- Date and time of testing
- Analyst name and signature
- Equipment identification numbers
- Reagent lot numbers and expiry dates
- All raw data and observations
- Calculations and final results
- Acceptance criteria and pass/fail determination

4.3.2 Reporting Timeline:
- Routine tests: Report results within 24 hours
- Urgent tests: Report results within 4 hours
- Microbiological tests: Report preliminary results in 24 hours, final results in 5-7 days
- Stability samples: Report results within 48 hours

4.3.3 Record Retention:
- Archive all test records for minimum 5 years
- Store in secure, climate-controlled area
- Maintain electronic backups of all data
- Ensure records are readily retrievable for audits

4.4 Out-of-Specification (OOS) Results

4.4.1 When OOS results are obtained:
- Stop testing and notify QC Manager immediately
- Do not discard sample or materials
- Document all circumstances surrounding the test
- Initiate OOS investigation within 24 hours
- Perform retest only after investigation approval

4.4.2 Investigation Requirements:
- Review all raw data and calculations
- Check equipment calibration status
- Verify reagent quality and expiry
- Interview analyst about testing conditions
- Document all findings in investigation report
- Determine root cause and corrective actions

5. EQUIPMENT AND MATERIALS

5.1 Required Equipment:
- Analytical balance (calibrated quarterly)
- pH meter (calibrated daily)
- UV-Vis spectrophotometer (qualified annually)
- HPLC system (qualified annually)
- Dissolution apparatus (calibrated semi-annually)
- Incubators (temperature mapped quarterly)
- Microscope (calibrated annually)

5.2 Reagents and Standards:
- Use only ACS grade or higher reagents
- Verify Certificate of Analysis for all standards
- Label all reagents with receipt date and expiry
- Store reagents according to manufacturer recommendations
- Prepare fresh solutions as specified in test methods

6. SAFETY REQUIREMENTS

6.1 Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):
- Laboratory coat (mandatory at all times)
- Safety glasses with side shields
- Nitrile gloves (change when contaminated)
- Closed-toe shoes
- Face shield when handling hazardous materials

6.2 Chemical Handling:
- Review Safety Data Sheets (SDS) before use
- Use fume hood for volatile or toxic chemicals
- Never pipette by mouth
- Label all containers clearly
- Dispose of chemical waste in designated containers

6.3 Incident Reporting:
- Report all spills, exposures, or injuries immediately
- Complete incident report within 24 hours
- Seek medical attention for any exposure
- Notify supervisor and safety officer

7. QUALITY CONTROL MEASURES

7.1 System Suitability:
- Perform system suitability tests before each analytical run
- Document all system suitability parameters
- Do not proceed with testing if system suitability fails
- Investigate and resolve failures before continuing

7.2 Control Samples:
- Analyze control samples with each batch
- Plot control results on control charts
- Investigate trends or out-of-control points
- Update control limits annually

7.3 Proficiency Testing:
- Participate in external proficiency testing programs
- Review and document all proficiency test results
- Investigate any unsatisfactory results
- Implement corrective actions as needed

8. TRAINING REQUIREMENTS

8.1 Initial Training:
- All new QC analysts must complete training on this SOP
- Training must include both theoretical and practical components
- Analysts must demonstrate competency before independent testing
- Document all training in training records

8.2 Ongoing Training:
- Annual refresher training on this SOP
- Training on any SOP revisions within 30 days
- Competency assessment annually
- Cross-training on multiple test methods

9. DEVIATIONS

9.1 Any deviation from this SOP must be:
- Documented immediately
- Reported to QC Manager
- Investigated for impact on results
- Approved by QA before continuing
- Included in batch documentation
